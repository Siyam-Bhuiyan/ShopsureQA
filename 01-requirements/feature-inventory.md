# Feature Inventory — automationexercise.com

**Source:** Live exploration of https://automationexercise.com on 2026-09-19 (page HTML fetched and inspected directly — form field names, `id`/`name` attributes, and status codes below were read from the actual markup, not assumed).

This inventory lists every distinct feature/page identified on the site, with its URL, a short description of what was actually found there, and a rough complexity rating for test design purposes.

| # | Feature | URL(s) | Description (from real exploration) | Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home Page | `/` | Rotating hero banner (3 slides), left sidebar with Category accordion (Women/Men/Kids) and Brands list with per-brand product counts, "Features Items" product grid, "recommended items" carousel, footer newsletter subscribe box. | Low |
| 2 | Product Listing (All Products) | `/products` | Grid of all products (image, name, price in Rs., "Add to cart", "View Product"), left sidebar with Category accordion and Brands list, text search box (`#search_product`). No visible pagination — all products load on one page. | Medium |
| 3 | Category Products | `/category_products/{id}` (e.g. `/category_products/1` = Women > Dress) | Product grid filtered by category, reached via sidebar links on Home/Products. Categories found: Women (Dress, Tops, Saree), Men (Tshirts, Jeans), Kids (Dress, Tops & Shirts). | Low |
| 4 | Brand Products | `/brand_products/{name}` (e.g. `/brand_products/Polo`) | Product grid filtered by brand. Brands found: Polo (6), H&M (5), Madame (5), Mast & Harbour (3), Babyhug (4), Allen Solly Junior (3), Kookie Kids (3), Biba (5) — counts as shown on site at time of testing. | Low |
| 5 | Product Search | Search box on `/products` (`input#search_product`, `name="search"`, placeholder "Search Product") → results rendered on `/products` | Client posts the search term and re-renders the product grid with matches (and a "Searched Products" heading, per Test Case 9). | 7 | Shopping Cart | `/view_cart` | Cart items table (Item/Description/Price/Quantity/Total columns, confirmed from checkout page markup) or, when empty, a "Cart is empty! Click here to buy products." message. Footer newsletter subscribe box also present here (Test Case 11). | Medium |
| 8 | Signup — Step 1 (name/email capture) | `/login` (form `action="/signup"`, `method="POST"`) | Two fields only: `name` (text, required) and `email` (email, required), plus a hidden `form_type=signup` field. Submitting redirects into the full "Enter Account Information" step. | Medium |
| 8b | Signup — Step 2 (Account Information) | `/signup` (dynamic, POST-only) | Full account form. Logout is a simple link that ends the session. | Low-Medium |
| 10 | Checkout | `/checkout` | Shows "Your delivery address" / "Your billing address" blocks, an order review table, an optional order-comment `<textarea name="message">`, and a "Place Order" link to `/payment`. **Confirmed by direct request: this URL renders (HTTP 200) even with an empty cart and no logged-in session**, showing blank addresses and "Rs. 0" instead of redirecting — see Ambiguities in `requirements-analysis.md`. | High |

| 12 | Contact Us | `/contact_us` | Form (`#contact-us-form`): `name` (text, **not** HTML-required), `email` (email, required), `subject` (text, not required), `message` (textarea, **not** required despite being the main content field), `upload_file` (file input, **no `accept` or size constraint** in the markup). | 13 | Newsletter Subscription | Footer widget on every page (Home, Products, Cart, Contact, Checkout, Payment, etc.) | Single email field. **Real markup quirk confirmed on every page fetched:** the input's `id` is misspelled `susbscribe_email` (not `subscribe_email`) and the `<form>` reuses `class="searchform"` — a copy-paste artifact in the site's own HTML, worth knowing since automation locators must match the actual (misspelled) `id`. Success message: "You have been successfully subscribed!" | Low |
| 14 | Test Cases (reference page) | `/test_cases` | Static page listing 26 official test-case scenarios the site is designed to support (Register User, Login, Contact Us Form, Search Product, Place Order variants, etc. — full list read directly from the page, see `requirements-analysis.md`). | 15 | API Testing (reference + live API) | `/api_list` | Documentation page listing 14 live REST endpoints (products, brands, search, login verification, account CRUD) with method, path, required params, and documented response codes — read directly from the page. Feeds Phase 5. | Medium |

## Notes on exploration method
- Form structure (field `name`/`id`/`type`/`required`/`placeholder` attributes) was read from the raw HTML of `/login`, `/contact_us`, `/product_details/1`, `/checkout`, `/payment`, `/view_cart`, `/products`, and `/` — not inferred from rendered screenshots or assumed from prior knowledge of the site.
- The Signup Step-2 field list was cross-verified against the site's own `POST /api/createAccount` documentation on `/api_list`, since simulating the raw browser POST for that step is a multi-step, session-bound flow; the API docs give the authoritative field set for the same underlying form.
- One incidental finding while probing the signup POST (a request missing the hidden `form_type` field returned a full Django debug traceback, HTTP 500, `DEBUG=True`) is recorded as an observation in `requirements-analysis.md` — it was surfaced passively by a normal, non-malicious request, not by exploiting anything.
